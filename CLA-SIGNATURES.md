# Contributor License Agreement signatures

Everyone who contributes to Camera Bridge signs the [Individual CLA](CLA.md) once by adding **one line** to the table
below in their first pull request. A pull request from someone who is not in this list cannot be merged.

Add your line at the end of the table, in this exact format (one row, nothing else changed in this file):

```
| Your Name | your-github-username | The email in your git commits | YYYY-MM-DD | 1.0 |
```

By adding the line you confirm that you have read [CLA.md](CLA.md), that you agree to it, and that you are the person
named. The name and GitHub username must match the pull request's author. The email must be the one your commits use,
so a commit can be matched to your signature. (The Owner, Corey Silvia, owns the Project and does not need to sign.)
