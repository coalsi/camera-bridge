# Camera Bridge brand (October 2026)

## Logo

`camera-bridge-logo.svg` (200 × 200 viewBox): the Golden Gate Bridge seen through a camera lens. A covered-bridge red ring, a near-black lens, a
cream suspension bridge (Art Deco towers with crossbeams, one main cable, suspenders, deck), a deep-red inner rim and a faint cream glint.

At small sizes (32 px and below: favicons, menu bar), use a simplified variant: thicker cable and deck, towers without the small crossbeams,
three to five suspenders, no glint. The menu bar template is a single-colour (black, alpha) version of the same shape.

## Colours

The red is the barn red of New England's painted covered bridges (West Cornwall, Connecticut; Vermont's red bridges).

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bridge red | `#9E2B20` | The brand colour: logo ring, primary buttons and their fills, selected states, focus accents on light backgrounds, links on light backgrounds |
| Deep red | `#6E1A14` | Pressed and hover-darker states, the logo's inner rim, roof-dark details |
| Light red | `#E06A5A` | Links, accent text and icons on dark backgrounds (bridge red is too dark to read on near-black) |
| Lighter red (hover on dark) | `#EC8577` | Hover of light-red links on dark |
| Cream | `#EFE6D8` | Text and icons on bridge-red fills, the logo's bridge and lens rings |
| Lens black | `#151517` | The logo's lens; matches the dark theme's page background |

Replace every amber (`#e8a33d`, `#f0b254`, `#1a1407` "on amber", and their tints) with these: amber fills become bridge red with cream text;
amber text or icons on dark become light red; amber on light backgrounds becomes bridge red. Keep status colours that mean something
(live green, recording red `#ff6a61`, warnings): if a status red sits next to brand red and they could be confused, keep the status red as is
and make sure the brand red is never used to mean "recording" or "error".

Contrast (WCAG AA): cream on bridge red ≈ 6.0 : 1; light red on `#151517` ≈ 5.5 : 1; bridge red on white ≈ 7.5 : 1.
